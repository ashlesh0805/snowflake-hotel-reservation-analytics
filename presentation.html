Business Context

Hotel Operations Scenario & Key Pain Points

A multi-property hotel chain needed a reliable, modern cloud warehouse to replace fragmented reports and eliminate revenue leakage.

1. Anomalous Booking Dates

Partner OTAs and direct systems frequently feed corrupted dates where check-out precedes check-in or zero-day stays occur, artificially skewing length-of-stay and revenue KPIs.

2. Untracked Cancellation Losses

Cancellations across different channels (Website, OTA, Phone, Walk-in) caused hidden revenue losses with zero visibility into refund timelines or exposure by room type.

3. Dynamic Occupancy Calculation

Measuring occupancy requires reconciling active room counts against fluctuating days in calendar months while isolating cancelled bookings so available inventory isn't miscounted.

4. Manual Aggregation & Monitoring

Executive rollups previously required tedious manual spreadsheet formulas with no automated scheduling, time travel audit capability, or health monitoring.

Data Quality

Automated Date Validation & Anomaly Detection

Intercepting corrupted records at the Clean layer ensures downstream financial and operational reports are 100% accurate.

Validation Logic in Snowflake SQL

CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW V_RESERVATION_DATE_VALIDATION AS
SELECT
  reservation_id, check_in_date, check_out_date,
  DATEDIFF('day', check_in_date, check_out_date) 
    AS stay_duration_days,
  CASE
    WHEN check_in_date IS NULL 
      THEN 'INVALID - NULL dates'
    WHEN check_out_date < check_in_date 
      THEN 'INVALID - Checkout before Checkin'
    WHEN check_out_date = check_in_date 
      THEN 'INVALID - Zero night stay'
    ELSE 'VALID'
  END AS date_validation_status
FROM RAW_RESERVATIONS;

Anomalies Intercepted & Excluded

RES041 (Grand Palace Hotel)

Check-in: 2024-11-15 | Check-out: 2024-11-10 (-5 days). Flagged as checkout before checkin.

RES042 (Seaside Resort)

Check-in: 2024-12-25 | Check-out: 2024-12-20 (-5 days). Filtered out from room availability.

RES043 (Mountain Lodge)

Check-in: 2024-10-10 | Check-out: 2024-10-10 (0 days). Zero-night stay excluded from occupancy.
