02
PROBLEM STATEMENT

Core Operational Challenges in Multi-Property Hospitality

Fragmented booking sources introduced corrupt dates, unmonitored cancellation exposure, and inaccurate occupancy metrics.

01   Anomalous Date Corruptions
Check-out Preceding Check-in
Corrupt booking records where check-out precedes check-in or zero-night stays severely skew length-of-stay, room availability, and inventory calculations.
02   Untracked Cancellation Leakage
Revenue Loss & Sudden Spikes
Sudden cancellation spikes through indirect channels caused unmonitored revenue leakage without clear visibility into refund volumes ($5,290+).
03   Dynamic Occupancy Complexity
Fluctuating Monthly Capacity
Reconciling active room capacity across varying calendar days while excluding cancelled bookings required dynamic cross-join modeling.
04   Manual Pipeline Vulnerability
Spreadsheet Silos & Idle Compute
Relying on end-of-month manual spreadsheet exports risked human error, lacked versioning, and lacked automated schedule health monitoring.

DATA QUALITY & INTEGRITY

Reservation Date Validation Logic & Quarantine

Automated SQL rules quarantine negative durations and zero-night stays before data reaches analytics.

SQL Automated Validation Rules
Deterministic Verification Case Statement
CASE
  WHEN check_in_date IS NULL 
    THEN 'INVALID - NULL dates'

  WHEN check_out_date < check_in_date
    THEN 'INVALID - Checkout before Checkin'

  WHEN check_out_date = check_in_date
    THEN 'INVALID - Zero night stay'

  WHEN DATEDIFF('day', check_in, check_out) > 365
    THEN 'WARNING - Stay exceeds 365 days'

  ELSE 'VALID'
END AS date_validation_status;

Production Impact: Any record not evaluating to 'VALID' is blocked from entering the production CLEAN table, safeguarding financial models.

Quarantined Anomalies (3 Corrupt Bookings)
Detected & Isolated in Clean View
RES041 • Grand Palace Hotel
IN: 2024-11-15  |  OUT: 2024-11-10 (-5 Days)
Check-out occurs 5 days before check-in. Flagged immediately by validation view and filtered out of CLEAN_RESERVATIONS.
RES042 • Seaside Resort
IN: 2024-12-25  |  OUT: 2024-12-20 (-5 Days)
OTA booking injection with inverted calendar sequence. Quarantined to prevent skewing room availability metrics.
RES043 • Mountain Lodge
IN: 2024-10-10  |  OUT: 2024-10-10 (0 Nights)
Same-day booking and departure without overnight stay. Excluded from overnight occupancy percentage calculations.
